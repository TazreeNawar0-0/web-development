~Jupiter~

Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system.

Key Facts About Jupiter: Jupiter is so huge that more than 1,300 Earths could fit inside it, and its mass is 2.5 times greater than all the other planets in the solar system combined. Despite its massive size, Jupiter has the shortest day in the solar system, completing one full rotation on its axis in just about 9.9 to 10 hours. This rapid spin causes it to bulge at the equator and flatten at the poles. Though not as bright or famous as Saturn’s, Jupiter actually has a faint system of rings made mostly of dust particles kicked up by meteorite impacts on its inner moons.

The Great Red Spot: Jupiter's iconic giant red storm has been raging for hundreds of years. It is large enough to swallow Earth whole, though it has been slowly shrinking over time.
